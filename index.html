Why the Conspiracy

More than
the next pull.

Conspiracy is built around what happens when people gather around a table: learning a new game, starting a campaign, meeting another player, and staying for one more round.

The tables make room for players, storytellers and curious newcomers. Whether you arrive with a group or no plan at all, the goal is the same: leave with a better story than the one you walked in with.

Before you come

Good questions.

A few quick answers for players, groups and first-time visitors.

Do you host events and campaigns?

Yes. Check the Events page for the latest game nights, tournaments, campaigns and community gatherings.

Can I bring my own group?

Absolutely. Reach out before your visit so the team can help you plan space and timing.

Do you sell Conspiracy merch?

Yes. Visit the Merch page on the original site for current shirts, hoodies and stickers.

Do I need to book a table or room?

For a specific table, room or group plan, message the crew first so they can check what is open before you come over.

How do I find the next event?

Check the Events page for the latest calendar and confirmed event details.
